Day 5 · flexible distance, flat former railway line · minimal elevation gain

Ben Macdui ★★★★☆

Day 8 · ~9–10.5 miles round trip from Cairngorm ski centre · ~700m elevation gain · 7–9 hours hiking

Why it's hard: it's the second-highest peak in the UK, the plateau is exposed with little shelter, and in November there's real potential for snow, ice, and short daylight — this isn't just a long walk, it's genuine winter mountain terrain.

What it takes: full day (10–12 hrs total once you add the 1.5–2 hr drive from Kirkmichael each way), warm waterproof layers, headlamp for the early start, proper hiking boots, and ideally trekking poles. Check conditions the day before — this is not a hike to do in bad weather.

Lairig Ghru ★★★★★

Day 8 alt · ~19 miles point-to-point (Aviemore ↔ Braemar) · significant elevation, remote terrain

Why it's hard: it's over twice the distance of Ben Macdui, it's a one-way route through the most remote part of the Cairngorms (not a loop), and it climbs to 835m at the pass itself — help is genuinely far away for most of the route.

What it takes: everything Ben Macdui needs, plus a car shuttle or taxi arranged at the far end since you can't walk back the way you came in one day. Only worth considering if that logistics piece is already sorted — otherwise Ben Macdui is the simpler, still-serious option.

Easier alternatives / backups

Not currently on the itinerary — good swaps if anyone wants a gentler day, or if weather/recovery timelines change plans.

Stanage Edge ★☆☆☆☆

Easier alternative

~3–4 miles along the ridge top, 2–3 hours. Wide, well-trodden path with some uneven rocky sections but no scrambling. Shorter loop options available if you want even less. No poles or special gear needed — just decent shoes, since the top is windier and colder than the valley below.

📍 Parking

Coach and Horses Rock Formation ★☆☆☆☆

Easier alternative

Also called the Wheelstones, on Derwent Edge. ~2.5–3 miles from the roadside layby near Ladybower Reservoir, moderate but well-marked, no climbing needed to see the rocks up close. Note: this is over near Ladybower, a bit further from Tideswell than Stanage, so it adds more driving if pairing both in one day.
