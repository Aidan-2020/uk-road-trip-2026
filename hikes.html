🥾 Hikes

Distance, elevation, parking — plan ahead

Mam Tor ★★☆☆☆

Day 4 · ~3.5 miles round trip · ~275m elevation gain · ~2.5 hours

Well-maintained paved path most of the way, ridge walk with big views. Good for a first-day-hiking warm-up. Free parking at Mam Nick car park, gets busy on weekends.

Monsal Trail ★☆☆☆☆

Day 5 · flexible distance, flat former railway line · minimal elevation gain

Easy, flat, and scenic — good for a lower-effort afternoon after Chatsworth and Bakewell. Runs through old railway tunnels. Multiple car parks along the route (Bakewell, Monsal Head, Millers Dale).

Glenshee small hike ★★☆☆☆

Day 7 · 3–5 miles · moderate elevation depending on route

Warm-up hike for the Highlands, near the ski centre. Nothing technical — good for shaking off travel legs before the big one.

Ben Macdui ★★★★☆

Day 8 · ~9–10.5 miles round trip from Cairngorm ski centre · ~700m elevation gain · 7–9 hours hiking

Second-highest peak in the UK (1,309m). Standard route starts at Cairngorm ski centre car park, ~1.5–2 hrs' drive from Kirkmichael each way. In November, expect short daylight and possible snow/ice at altitude — check conditions and bring proper winter gear, not just warm layers.

Lairig Ghru ★★★★★

Day 8 alt · ~19 miles point-to-point (Aviemore ↔ Braemar) · significant elevation, remote terrain

A linear through-route, not an out-and-back — needs a car shuttle or taxi arranged at the far end to do in one day. Longer and more remote than Ben Macdui; only worth it if the logistics are sorted in advance.
