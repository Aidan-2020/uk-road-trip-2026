Day 8 — BIG HIKE

Ben Macdui or Lairig Ghru

Hiking7–9 hrs Difficulty★★★★☆
Before sunrise

Pack up

Sandwiches, water, layers.

Early

Ben Macdui

Expect 7–9 hours of hiking. The usual trailhead is the Cairngorm ski centre car park near Aviemore — about 1.5–2 hours' drive from Kirkmichael each way, so budget more like 10–12 hours total for the day, not just the hike time.

Drive + hike combined 📍 Directions
Alt option

Lairig Ghru

This is a linear route between Aviemore and Braemar, not an out-and-back — doing the whole thing in a day means arranging a car shuttle or taxi at the far end. Ben Macdui is the simpler choice unless that's sorted.

Needs shuttle logistics
